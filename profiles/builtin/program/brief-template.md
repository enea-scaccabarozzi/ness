# Brief: <vertical id> <title>

Copy this file to `briefs/<vertical>.md`; a brief does not promote side remarks to rules.

## Question
The one question this child answers or the one thing it builds, with its limits; no candidate solutions.

## Outcome
What is true when the child is done, checkable by someone else.

## Invariants and doctrine
The root invariants and the doctrine entries (P<n>, G<n>) that bind this child, by id, verbatim.

## Operator words
What the operator said that applies, verbatim, by decision id.

## Sources
Where to read: files, specs, earlier briefs, survey results.

## Rights and limits
What the child may touch and what it must not. Autonomy level, from the program's Autonomy mode.

## Tier
The child's work size and model tier.

## Spec owner
Who writes the spec for this vertical: the root, the child or the operator.

## Integration target
The branch the child integrates into.

## How to report
A handoff with Done, Now, Next; choices and rejected alternatives in words; notes without ids.
